Settings

Trusted domains

Used by the sender-spoofing rule templates (display-name/brand mismatch, lookalike-domain check). Add your own organization's domain(s) so impersonation of your own company is caught too, not just public brands. A domain here also covers its subdomains (adding mycompany.com trusts mail.mycompany.com too). For more control, add a regex instead — one per line, written as /pattern/flags, e.g. /\.corp\.mycompany\.com$/.

Domain categories

"What type of domain is this?" — checked against the sender/reply-to/return-path domains, every URL domain in the body, and domain-like text in headers and attachment filenames. Ships with a small starter set; add your own keywords to any category, or create new ones — everything here stays in this browser, never in the app's own source. A keyword starting with . matches the domain's ending (a TLD/suffix, e.g. .xyz); anything else matches as a plain substring anywhere in the domain.
